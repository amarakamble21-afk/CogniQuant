      General care topics by condition

      Alzheimer’s disease

      Care may combine symptom support, planning, and—in some circumstances—specialist assessment for disease-modifying treatment.

      General topics to discuss with a care team

      • Medicines for symptoms and whether they fit the person’s health history.
      • For eligible people with early symptomatic disease and confirmed amyloid, whether an approved treatment such as lecanemab or donanemab is appropriate, including monitoring and risks.
      • Support for daily activities, safety, caregiver needs, and advance planning.

      MedlinePlus: Alzheimer’s disease
      FDA: lecanemab indication

      Parkinson’s disease

      Care is individualized around movement and non-movement symptoms, daily function, treatment response, and preferences.

      General topics to discuss with a care team

      • Symptom-relieving medicines, timing, benefits, and side effects.
      • Physical, occupational, or speech therapy and appropriate exercise support.
      • Whether specialist evaluation for procedures such as deep-brain stimulation is relevant.

      MedlinePlus: Parkinson’s disease
      NINDS: Parkinson’s disease

      Huntington’s disease

      Supportive care can address movement, mood, cognition, communication, nutrition, and changing daily needs.

      General topics to discuss with a care team

      • Options to manage specific symptoms and how benefits and risks will be reviewed.
      • Physical, occupational, speech, nutrition, and mental-health support.
      • Genetic counseling and family support when discussing genetic results.

      MedlinePlus: Huntington’s disease
      GeneReviews: Huntington disease

      Questions you could bring to an appointment

      • What does this result measure, and how reliable is it for my situation?
      • Could medications, testing conditions, or other health factors affect it?
      • What are the potential benefits, risks, alternatives, and monitoring needs of each option?
      • Which symptoms or changes should prompt me to contact the care team?
